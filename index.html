Common questions

How do I choose between Baofeng and Motorola?

Tell us your budget, how many radios you need, where you’ll use them and how many hours a day. We’ll recommend a model, and it’s fine if that turns out to be the cheaper one.

Do I need a licence to use a walkie talkie in Malaysia?

Radio equipment and radio frequencies are regulated in Malaysia by the MCMC. Some radios and frequencies need approval or an assignment. Ask us before you buy and we’ll tell you what we know, and you can confirm the current rules with MCMC.

Do you sell batteries, chargers and earpieces?

Yes. Send us a photo of your radio or its model name on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you what fits.

Can I buy several radios for my company?

Yes. Message us the quantity you need. We can prepare a quotation for your company.

How far can a walkie talkie reach?

It depends on the radio and the surroundings. Open ground gives the best range, while buildings, hills and thick jungle cut it down. Tell us where you’ll use it and we’ll be honest about what to expect.

Which areas do you serve?

We’re in Kuching and serve Petra Jaya, Kota Samarahan, Padawan and nearby areas. Contact us about delivery elsewhere in Sarawak.
